# Terminology Ledger Template

Each research project should maintain a terminology ledger to ensure consistency.
Copy this file to your project and fill in your specific terms.

## Format

| Term | Preferred form | Variants to avoid | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| example | MPC | mpc, model predictive control | Model Predictive Control |
